How We Organize Work

A guide for everyone at Justuju

The Idea

Work should start with a purpose and end with execution. A group decides why something needs to happen. Then the people with the right skills make it happen. Our system keeps these separate on purpose.

Branches — why does this work exist?

A branch is a strategic stream — something Justuju has collectively decided matters. Branches do not execute work directly. They initiate work. A branch says: "This matters. Let's make it happen."

  • Team Bonding — relationships and shared experiences
  • Collective Work — running the organization day-to-day
  • Networking, Outreach and Revenue Generation — connecting with the world, sustaining ourselves
  • Perspective Building — justice, equity, leadership
  • Systems and Policy Strengthening — organizational structures
  • Learning and Capacity Building — growing individual and collective skills

Each branch has an anchor (rotated every 3 months) — a facilitator and accountability point, not a manager.

Initiatives — what specifically are we doing?

An initiative is a concrete project or program — the bridge between a branch's purpose and a function's execution. Always owned by a branch, executed by one or more functions.

Example: CCL App Networking, Outreach and Revenue Generation

  • Software Engineering builds the application
  • Finance handles billing and payments
  • Administration manages vendor contracts

Each function picks up their part. Tasks live in each function's backlog, following each function's workflow.

The initiative has an owner (or small committee) who tracks progress across all involved functions.

Internal Work & Roles

Sustaining work: Sometimes a function has internal work (refactoring code, setting up filing systems, building shared infra). This is normal — it lives in the function's backlog, just not linked to any initiative.

Roles are accountability positions, not hierarchical.
